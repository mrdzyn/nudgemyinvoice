## Mandatory checkpoint

Before adding a new country/region:
1. verify the current store requirements;
2. identify local age/content-rating rules;
3. identify privacy/data-localization or consent requirements;
4. identify publishing, ICP, game, news, religious, medical, financial, or other sector permits where applicable;
5. identify local entity/publisher/operator requirements;
6. record the evidence/permit/reference and owner;
7. obtain specialist/legal review where the regulatory interpretation is material.

Do not infer global availability from a successful US/global-default store submission.
