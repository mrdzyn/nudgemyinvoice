# Age Suitability & Content Ratings

Age/content ratings are a release gate, not a marketing choice. Answer store questionnaires based on the actual content and capabilities in the shipped build.

## Common content/capability inventory

Review at minimum:
- cartoon/fantasy and realistic violence;
- profanity/crude humor;
- horror/fear;
- sexual/suggestive content or nudity;
- alcohol/tobacco/drugs;
- gambling/simulated gambling;
- loot boxes/chance-based activities;
- contests;
- user-generated content;
- messaging/chat/social features;
- unrestricted web access;
- advertising;
- medical/wellness content;
- parental controls;
- age assurance;
- other store-specific descriptors.

## Apple

Apple requires an age rating. Complete the current App Store Connect age-rating questionnaire and record region-specific implications. Apple can generate different regional ratings from the same answers.

Official:
- https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/set-an-app-age-rating
- https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions

Revisit the questionnaire when content/capabilities materially change.

## Google Play / IARC

Google Play uses the International Age Rating Coalition (IARC) content-rating system. Google requires apps to have a content rating and requires developers to accurately complete the Play Console questionnaire.

Official:
- https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898843

Record:
- IARC questionnaire completion date;
- certificate/reference if available;
- regional ratings returned;
- answers that depend on dynamic content, UGC, ads, gambling/chance mechanics, or social capabilities.

If app content/features change in a way that affects questionnaire answers, submit an updated questionnaire.

Do not assume an IARC result replaces a store/jurisdiction-specific requirement outside participating workflows.

## Microsoft Store / IARC

Microsoft Store also uses the International Age Rating Coalition questionnaire for Store ratings. Complete the current Partner Center/Microsoft Store IARC questionnaire accurately and keep the result aligned with the shipped content.

Official:
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/publish/store-policies

Record the questionnaire completion/reference and regional rating result where applicable.

## Children / minors

If the intended or actual audience includes children/minors, separately review:
- store Families/Kids requirements;
- age-assurance/parental-control requirements;
- child/minor privacy rules for target jurisdictions;
- ads/analytics/UGC/social restrictions;
- purchase/monetization safeguards.

Escalate jurisdiction-specific legal uncertainty rather than inferring compliance from an age rating alone.
